## 8. Chapter Body Content — Originality Requirements

The dissertation shall:
- Consist of the student's own account of investigations
- Be a record of original work OR ordered critical exposition of existing knowledge
- Be an **integrated whole** presenting a coherent argument
- Give critical assessment of relevant literature
- Describe the method of research and findings
- Include discussion on findings
- Include a **full bibliography and references**

## 10. References & Citations

**Format**: All references cited in text by **superscript number** (e.g., `[1]`)

### Papers / Journals
```
Author(s), "Title of paper," Journal Name, Year, Volume, Pages.
Example:
Thiel WJ and Nguyen LT, "Fluidized bed film coating...", J. Pharmacy, 1984, 36, 145-152.
```

### Conference Papers
```
Author(s), "Title," Conference Name, Location, Publisher, Year.
```

### Websites
```
Author, "Title," Month Year, URL
Example:
Boggs D, "Acceleration and Drift due to Gust forces," July 2009, www.example.com/paper.pdf
```

### Books
```
Author(s). Title; Edition; Publisher, City, Year, pp X–Y.
Example:
Pelzar MJ., Chan ECS., In Microbiology; 5th Edn; Tata McGraw Hill, New Delhi, 1993, pp 536.
```

### Dissertations
```
Author. Degree Dissertation, "Title," University, Month Year.
Example:
Vaishnav DK, PhD Dissertation, "...", Rashtriya Raksha University, July 2012.
```

### Patents
```
Author(s). Description. Patent Type and Number, Year.
```

> **Note**: No hyperlinks between superscripts in text and reference section. Superscripts only.

## 12. Plagiarism Requirements

| Property | Requirement |
|----------|-------------|
| Software | **Turnitin** or **iThenticate** or **Urkund** |
| Final check | Central Library performs final official check |
| Certificate needed | Plagiarism Verification Certificate (pasted at back of hardbound) |

**Allowed Exclusions during check:**
- Quotes
- Bibliography
- Phrases (small matches up to 10 words)
- Small similarity < 1%
- Mathematical formulas
- Names of Institutions, Departments

**Self-Plagiarism**: If published work appears as plagiarism — supervisor issues a Plagiarism Self Exclusion Certificate listing only those published articles.
